Suggested Classroom Uses

  • Monetary policy & the Fed's dual mandate: The Fed Policy Targets table shows real, current CPI, Core PCE, unemployment, GDP growth, and Fed Funds Rate readings against the Fed's own longer-run targets - a live example for teaching how the FOMC's Summary of Economic Projections works.
  • Fundamentals vs. market sentiment: The Fundamentals vs. Sentiment Gauge gives students a concrete, numeric way to separate "what the macro data explains" from "what's left over" in a sector's price move - a good jumping-off point for discussing market efficiency and behavioral finance.
  • Time value of money & loan mechanics: The Financial Calculators page (mortgage, auto loan, business valuation, real estate investment) shows students the actual amortization and DSCR math behind the numbers, not just a black-box payment figure.
  • Building financial vocabulary: The Glossary covers every indicator, index, and rate on the site, with what it measures and who publishes it - useful as an assigned reading or reference for an intro finance/econ course.
  • Real estate & property finance: The Property Manager and Real Estate pages work as a live case study for cap rate, cash-on-cash return, and DSCR concepts in a real estate finance course.

For Students

Everything on Stock Digest is free to explore on your own - no login required for most pages. If your professor sets up classroom access, ask them about any course-specific materials or discussion questions they're using alongside it. If you're studying independently and have a question about how a specific calculator or indicator works, the Glossary and the hover-over definitions throughout the site are a good place to start.
