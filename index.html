Fed Policy Targets

Compares each indicator's latest reading to the Federal Reserve's own longer-run target or projection (June 2026 SEP). Inflation targets (CPI, Core PCE) are the Fed's official, fixed 2% policy goal. Unemployment, GDP growth, and the federal funds "neutral rate" are NOT fixed targets - they're the median of individual FOMC members' own quarterly forecasts (the Summary of Economic Projections), and they move release to release; the color reflects distance from target only, not a judgment that above or below is "good," since that depends on the indicator and the Fed's mandate.
